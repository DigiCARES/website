The DigiCARES team is pleased to share that Dr. Chao Fan, a participating faculty member in the DigiCARES project, has been named a recipient of the 2026 College of Arts & Sciences Outstanding Faculty Achievement Award in Scholarship, Research, and Creative Activities at New Mexico State University.

This award recognizes faculty members for distinguished contributions to research and scholarly activities. Dr. Fan was nominated by a colleague and selected through a competitive evaluation process conducted by the College of Arts & Sciences Faculty Awards Committee, with final approval by the Dean.

The award includes a $1,500 honorarium and formal recognition at a college reception scheduled for April 28, 2026.

Dr. Fan’s research contributions align closely with the DigiCARES mission, particularly in advancing data-driven and AI-enabled approaches to complex societal challenges. His recognition reflects both individual excellence and the broader impact of collaborative research within the DigiCARES program.
