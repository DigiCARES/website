The DigiCARES project held its second annual meeting August 27–29 at The University of Alabama in Huntsville (UAH), bringing together researchers, students, project evaluators, and regional partners to review progress and strengthen cross-institutional collaboration. The meeting also welcomed new team members and congratulated DigiCARES researcher Fengyu Wang on promotion to Associate Professor.

DigiCARES—Accelerating Community-Centric Energy Transformation through AI-Driven Digital Twinning for Climate-Aware Resilience—is a $6 million National Science Foundation project led by New Mexico State University in partnership with UAH, Oklahoma State University, and Montana State University.

UAH Dean Jodi Price delivered opening remarks. Principal Investigator Di Shi, Project Manager Anthony Moye, and external evaluator Adrienne Celaya then provided an overview of project progress and priorities. Participating faculty researchers included Chao Fan, Xin Han, Shuang Zhao, Ying Zhang, Guoliang Fan, Faraz Dadgostari, and Fengyu Wang. Postdoctoral researchers Danlu Cai and Yu Sun also presented updates on their work.

Presentations highlighted advances in climate-hazard analysis, grid reliability, energy affordability, AI-enabled outage restoration, digital twins, and resilient power-system control. The team also discussed emerging applications of large language models and agentic AI for energy-system analytics, decision support, and community-centered resilience planning.

Students played a central role in the program, with presentations by Jakirul Prothan, Yiyang Zhang, Somayeh Mehri, Seyi Fanifosi, Joseph Owusu, Lina Hommel, and Dominick Choate.

The meeting included engagement with the Tennessee Valley Authority and cross-task planning on shared datasets, platform integration, coordinated publications, and priorities for the coming year. The team also plans to release several open-source datasets and research tools to support the broader research community and accelerate innovation in climate-resilient energy systems.

Through these activities, the DigiCARES team further strengthened its integrated approach to developing AI-driven, climate-aware, and community-centered solutions for resilient energy systems.
