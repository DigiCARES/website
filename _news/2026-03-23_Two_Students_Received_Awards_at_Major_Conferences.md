The DigiCARES team is pleased to highlight recent achievements by its students, who have received recognition for their research contributions at leading national conferences.

At the 2026 AI x Energy Summit, Seyi Fanifosi was awarded the Best Poster Award for his presentation titled “Uncertainty-Aware Deep Reinforcement Learning for Robust Autonomous Voltage Control.” The summit brought together researchers, industry leaders, and policymakers working at the intersection of artificial intelligence and energy systems. His work drew significant interest for its focus on reinforcement learning–based voltage control and the incorporation of uncertainty quantification to enhance reliability and operator trust in real-time grid applications. Seyi is advised by Dr. Di Shi.

At the 2026 Annual Meeting of the American Association of Geographers (AAG) in San Francisco, Jakirul Islam Prothan received 2nd Place in the Remote Sensing Specialty Group Student Illustrated Paper Competition, recognizing the quality and impact of his research in remote sensing and geospatial analysis. Jakirul is advised by Dr. Chao Fan.

These accomplishments reflect the strength of the DigiCARES program in fostering high-impact, interdisciplinary research and supporting student success across domains such as AI-enabled energy systems and geospatial analytics.
